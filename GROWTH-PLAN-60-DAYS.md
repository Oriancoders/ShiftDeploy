# 60-day plan: close the next 10 clients

Start: Mon 28 Sep 2026. End: Fri 27 Nov 2026.
Owners: Muhammad Ali (delivery, demos, set-up) and Samiullah (outreach, follow-up, closing). Both do demos.

## The offer (one offer, not eight)

Lead with the problem every prospect already feels: **missed calls and slow replies**.

- **What:** AI receptionist that answers calls, WhatsApp and website chat, books into their diary, texts them every enquiry.
- **Pilot:** set up in 48 hours on their real number, free for 14 days. At the end we show them the calls it answered and bookings it made.
- **After the pilot:** one set-up fee plus a fixed monthly price, monthly rolling. Websites, SEO and automation are upsells later, not part of the first sale.
- **For clinics:** Review Your Doctor 30-day free trial as a second door-opener.

Why this offer: the pilot proves value with their own calls, so the close is “keep it or switch it off”, not “trust us”.

Pick 3 cities to start (for example Manchester, Leeds, Birmingham). Same pitch, same case study, local examples.

## The hook that gets replies: the missed-call audit

Ring each target business twice (lunchtime and after 5.30pm). Note whether anyone answered and whether voicemail was on. It’s an honest mystery-shop and it makes the email personal:

> Subject: Rang Riverside Dental at 6.10pm yesterday
>
> Hi Sarah, I rang the practice at 1.15pm and 6.10pm yesterday as a potential new patient. Both went to voicemail. Most new patients don’t leave one; they ring the next practice.
>
> We set up an AI receptionist that answers every call and WhatsApp and books straight into your diary. It takes 48 hours to set up, and you can try it free for 14 days on your own number.
>
> Worth a 15-minute look this week?
>
> Samiullah, Co-Founder, ShiftDeploy

Only report what actually happened on the call. Never exaggerate the result.

# Google Search Console and Bing: step-by-step

Why now: for weeks the Vercel firewall challenged crawlers, so Google may have seen 403 errors on robots.txt and the sitemap. The firewall is now off (Bot Protection off, AI bots allowed). These steps tell Google to come back and re-read everything.

## Today (about 30 minutes)

1. **Check the property.** In Search Console, use a **Domain property** for `shiftdeploy.com` (verified by a DNS TXT record in Cloudflare). It covers www, non-www, http and https in one place.
2. **Submit the sitemap.** Indexing → Sitemaps → enter `sitemap.xml` → Submit. Remove any old sitemap entries (for example from the old Vite site). Status should read “Success”.
   - The sitemap updates itself. Each scheduled blog post is added on its publish day, so you never need to resubmit it.
3. **Request indexing for the key pages** (URL Inspection → paste URL → Request indexing; Google allows roughly 10 a day):
   - https://shiftdeploy.com/
   - https://shiftdeploy.com/digital-receptionist
   - https://shiftdeploy.com/services
   - https://shiftdeploy.com/services/shiftbuild
   - https://shiftdeploy.com/services/shiftspeed
   - https://shiftdeploy.com/services/shiftconvert
   - https://shiftdeploy.com/services/shiftflow
   - https://shiftdeploy.com/insights/whatsapp-business-api-guide
   - https://shiftdeploy.com/about
   - https://shiftdeploy.com/insights
4. **Fix the firewall errors.** Indexing → Pages. Look for “Blocked due to access forbidden (403)” or “Server error (5xx)”. Open each reason and click **Validate fix**.
5. **Check robots.txt.** Settings → robots.txt report → make sure the latest fetch is “Fetched”, not an error. If it shows an error, click “Request a recrawl”.

## Tomorrow (when the PageSpeed quota resets)

6. Test https://pagespeed.web.dev for the home page and /digital-receptionist, mobile and desktop. Screenshot the scores for the record.

## Bing Webmaster Tools (15 minutes, important for AI search)

ChatGPT search and Microsoft Copilot rely heavily on Bing’s index, so this matters for AI SEO.

7. Go to bing.com/webmasters → **Import from Google Search Console**. It copies the site and sitemap in one step.
8. Submit `https://shiftdeploy.com/sitemap.xml` if it isn’t imported.
9. Use URL Submission for the same 10 key pages.

## On every blog publish day (4 a week, 5 minutes)

10. Run `node scripts/blog/status.mjs` to see what went live.
11. URL Inspection → paste the new post URL → Request indexing (Google and Bing).
12. Share it on LinkedIn from Samiullah’s or Muhammad Ali’s profile (whoever is the author).

## Every Monday (15 minutes)

13. **Performance report:** filter Country = United Kingdom, last 28 days. Note clicks, impressions and average position.
14. **Queries:** find queries with impressions but position 8 to 20. Those pages are close; improve their title or add an FAQ answering that exact query.
15. **Pages report:** make sure new posts move from “Discovered” to “Indexed”. If a post stays “Crawled, currently not indexed” for 3+ weeks, improve it (more specific examples, better internal links).
16. **Enhancements:** check the FAQ, Breadcrumbs and Article structured data reports for errors.

## Monthly

17. **Crawl stats** (Settings): host status should be green for robots.txt, DNS and server connectivity. Any 403 spike means the firewall is blocking again.
18. **Links:** check which pages have the fewest internal links and link to them from related posts.
19. After registering the company, create a **Google Business Profile** (service-area business, hide the address) and link it to the site.
