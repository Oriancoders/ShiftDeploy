# 100 blog keywords that can turn into leads (26 September 2026)

Every keyword below was confirmed on **UK Google Autocomplete** (gl=uk, en-GB), which means real people type it.
Keywords were chosen when the searcher is a **business owner with a problem we fix**, not a customer or a job seeker.
Relative demand comes from Google Trends (GB, last 12 months) where it was measured.

**Stage** says how close the searcher is to buying:
- **Buy**: comparing options or checking prices. Write these first; they convert best.
- **Problem**: knows the pain, not the fix yet. Good for leads when the post ends with a free check.
- **Learn**: early research. Builds traffic and trust, fewer direct leads.

**Page** is where the blog post should link, so the reader lands on the matching service.

Site rule: keep "UK" and "small business" out of visible headings where the keyword allows. Put them in the SEO title and description instead.

## How to write each post so it closes leads

1. **Answer the question in the first two sentences.** Google and AI search quote this part.
2. **Use the keyword in the title, the first paragraph and one H2.** Don’t repeat it more than that.
3. **Give real numbers**: prices, time saved, a worked example. Posts with a price range win "cost" searches.
4. **One FAQ block** with 4–6 questions taken from Google Autocomplete for that keyword.
5. **End with the free check**, linked to the matching service page above.
6. **Industry posts** ("for dentists", "for plumbers") should use that industry’s words and examples, not generic ones.
